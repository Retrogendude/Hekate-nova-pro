## Full historical notice

The instructions and paths below relate to this version's archive. They do not automatically describe the latest version. The limitations and validations remain those stated at the time.

HEKATE 6.5.4 / NYX 1.9.4 - NOVA, VERSION 10

NOVA
This version integrates the Nova presentation from the mockup: tabs at the
top, solid anthracite/blue cards, the same thin Lucide icons, 8-pixel corners
and an 18-pixel selection halo. The home screen has its
large Start card and four secondary accesses. Tools and Info
use four columns; Diagnostic presents its twelve categories.
The bottom banner gives the detail of the selected card.

The pages have been adapted to the console's 1280 x 720 screen. The data,
the availability of the tools and the chosen font depend on the device.
The previews provided are produced by the real LVGL engine on computer,
with demonstration values for the time, the temperature and the battery.

INSTALLATION
1. Keep a copy of the current bootloader folder.
2. Merge bootloader and fonts with the root of the SD card.
3. Launch hekate_ctcaer_6.5.4_custom.bin with the usual method.
No nyx.ini nor hekate_ipl.ini is provided in the archive.

NAVIGATION
- The five main tabs are at the top.
- In Tools, Info and Diagnostic, touching a card selects it.
  The arrow at the bottom right opens the selected function.
  Touching the text of the banner displays the full help.
- The home screen cards open their functions directly.
- Touching HEKATE at the top left opens the quick commands: Nyx
  settings, reload, restart and power off. A long press opens the credits.
- In Settings, touching System options opens the boot settings,
  including Auto Boot, delay, backlight and Reboot 2 Payload.
- Touching Appearance and reading opens the detailed Nyx settings.

LANGUAGES, FONTS AND EFFECTS
The language codes, the Font line and the sizes open the corresponding
selector. Save and reload applies the choices; Close cancels
the provisional choices. The seven languages and the nine sizes from 20 to 36
remain available. The size adapts the different typographic roles.
The three fonts provided are located in fonts at the root of the SD.
For a personal TTF/OTF font, use outils-polices to produce
a .nyxf file, then copy it into fonts and reload Nyx.

The Selection effects setting enables or disables the fixed halo of the icons.
The Save button appears when the system options are modified.
The background animations remain in Appearance > Effect. The reference
rendering uses the Disabled background effect and the Tiles home screen. The Carousel remains
available in Appearance > Layout, with the same Nova visual language.

DIAGNOSTIC AND SMOOTHNESS
The 42 native probes are compiled. The RAM test remains a separate service
with return to Hekate; keep hwtest_ram.bin and ram-ui-*.bin together
in bootloader/sys. Selecting a card launches no test.
The confirmations and guards of the original actions are kept.

The display, text and font optimisations are kept.
The fonts of the Nova roles are cached. The halo creates no
permanent animation. The background effects suspend during gestures
and diagnostics. The measurements provided concern the computer and do not
constitute an FPS measurement on console. The physical trials of
this version remain to be carried out.
