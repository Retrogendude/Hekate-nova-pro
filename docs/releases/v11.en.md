## Full historical notice

The instructions and paths below relate to this version's archive. They do not automatically describe the latest version. The limitations and validations remain those stated at the time.

HEKATE NOVA PRO - CUSTOMISED VERSION 11
Hekate 6.5.4 / Nyx 1.9.4

INSTALLATION
1. Keep a copy of the bootloader folder and of the current settings.
2. Merge bootloader, fonts and wallpapers with the root of the SD card.
3. Use the NEW payload hekate_nova_pro_6.5.4_v11.bin with the usual
   launch method. This is necessary to see the new startup.
   bootloader/update.bin contains exactly this same payload.
No nyx.ini or hekate_ipl.ini is provided: the existing settings remain
in place and the new options take their default Nova values.

THE TWELVE MODIFICATIONS
- The battery percentage stays on the right, next to its icon, even after
  the sensor refreshes.
- The five tabs are on the left; the four icons on the right open the
  Nyx settings, reload, restart or power off. The confirmations of the
  system commands are kept.
- A reflection travels along the outlines of the selection and its icon. These are
  short luminous segments, without blurred shadow and without background animation.
- The Nyx 1.9.4, 8 tools, 42 controls, Comfort and clarity banners are removed.
- The windows use the Nova colours, cards, outlines and spacings.
  Backup/restore, USB, emuMMC and the diagnostic pages use
  new layouts; the original technical controls are kept.
- Tools, Info, Diagnostic: first press = selection, second press on the
  same tile = opening. The opening arrow is removed. A long press
  keeps the special action of the tile when it already had one.
- Nyx Settings > Themes and colours: colours independent of the background,
  the text, the outlines and the reflection; preview before saving.
- The submenus extend the identity of the original Nova mockup.
- The startup displays Hekate Nova Pro with a new animated bar.
- The new logo is an N monogram in an open hexagon with a star.
- A fixed wallpaper can be chosen; the backgrounds of the menus and of the icons
  have two distinct opacity settings. The texts and symbols remain opaque.
- The background animations (waves, particles, aurora) and their settings are
  removed. The old INI fields remain compatible and have no effect
  on the display. The selection reflection remains enableable/disableable.

WALLPAPERS AND TRANSPARENCY
Copy the images into wallpapers at the root of the SD. The provided folder
contains Nova-Carbon.bmp, an original fixed background. Then choose Nyx Settings
> Themes and colours > Background and transparency, then Save and reload.
Close cancels the provisional choices. No wallpaper is enabled by default.
0% means transparent; 100% means opaque.

Formats: uncompressed BMP, 24 or 32 bits, up to 1920 x 1080. The cropping
and the scaling are done only once when loading. The selector
presents at most 32 files with a simple name, without accents or subfolder.
For a PNG/JPG image, drag the file onto outils-fonds/convertir-fond.cmd.
Python and Pillow are needed for this conversion on computer.

LANGUAGES, FONTS AND LAYOUT
The seven languages and the fonts remain accessible in Appearance.
The .nyxf font files go into fonts at the root of the SD.
The outils-polices folder contains the TTF/OTF converter.
The nine sizes from 20 to 36 remain available. The carousel is kept.
The reference rendering uses Tiles, Reading, 30px and opaque backgrounds.

DIAGNOSTICS
The 42 native probes are compiled; the submenus and results use
paged cards. The test names are legible; the technical identifiers
remain in the reports. No test starts on the first press
on a category of the main menu.
The RAM test is a separate service with Hekate return. Keep hwtest_ram.bin,
the seven ram-ui-*.bin and update.bin in the locations provided.

VALIDATION
The previews come from the real LVGL engine run on computer, with
demonstration data for the time, the temperature, the battery and the
hardware. The software checks are provided in verification and the
sources are included. The measurements are not FPS measured on Switch.
The physical trials of this version on console remain to be carried out.
