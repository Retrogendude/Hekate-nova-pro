HEKATE NOVA PRO - VERSION 14
Hekate 6.5.4 / Nyx 1.9.4

WIDER, SOFTER AND FASTER REFLECTION
- 129-pixel white band, about three times wider.
- Progressive gradient down to transparency at both ends.
- Travel in about 0.8 second, against 1.2 seconds previously.
- Still across the whole tile; the outlines remain fixed.

INSTALLATION
1. Back up the current files and settings of the SD card.
2. Merge bootloader and replace bootloader/sys/nyx.bin with the provided one.
3. Use hekate_nova_pro_6.5.4_v14.bin with the usual method.
   bootloader/update.bin contains exactly this same payload.
No nyx.ini or hekate_ipl.ini is provided. The settings are kept.
The fix for the menus with wallpaper and the original time remain in place.
Selection effects allows the reflection to be disabled.

Compilation and checks of the real rendering and of the navigation on PC carried out.
Smoothness to be confirmed on Switch: no console FPS measurement carried out here.
See documentation/CORRECTIONS-NOVA-PRO-V14.md and verification/.
