HEKATE NOVA PRO - VERSION 15
Hekate 6.5.4 / Nyx 1.9.4

FIXES
- Font of the technical reports, eMMC alignment and pagination of the tables.
- Translations of the backups/restores and of their logs.
- Cumulative preview of the colours before saving.
- Reflection across the whole tile: 0.48-second travel, fixed outlines.
- Progressive introduction bar up to 100%.
- Aligned emuMMC columns and files by pages without vertical list.
- Submenus and windows re-centred between the two banners.
- Font converter fixed for Caviar and the accents.

INSTALLATION
1. Back up the current files and settings of the SD card.
2. Merge the provided bootloader folder with the one of the card.
3. Use hekate_nova_pro_6.5.4_v15.bin with the usual method.
   bootloader/update.bin contains exactly this same payload.
Replace Nyx AND the payload to obtain all the boot modifications.
No nyx.ini or hekate_ipl.ini is provided. The settings are kept.
The first original time setting with language choice is kept.
Selection effects still allows the reflection to be disabled.

Compilation and verifications of the graphics engine and of the navigation on PC.
The data of the previews are simulated; no hardware test on Switch here.
Legibility, real startup and smoothness remain to be confirmed on your console.
See documentation/CORRECTIONS-NOVA-PRO-V15.md and verification/.
