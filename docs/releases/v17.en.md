## Full historical notice

The instructions and paths below relate to this version's archive. They do not automatically describe the latest version. The limitations and validations remain those stated at the time.

HEKATE NOVA PRO 17
Base: Hekate 6.5.4 / Nyx 1.9.4

UPDATE
Copy the contents of the package onto the SD card, keeping bootloader,
fonts and wallpapers at the root. Inject hekate_nova_pro_6.5.4_v17.bin.
The nyx.ini and hekate_ipl.ini files are not provided: your existing preferences
and boot profiles are kept.

CUSTOMISATION
Nyx Settings > Themes and colours offers four tabs: colours,
background and transparency, boot, Launch tile. The rectangular selector
keeps the changes and their preview when moving from one target to another.
The colours of the logo and of the boot bar are independent.
The BMP images 24/32 bit, uncompressed, of 1920 x 1080 maximum are
read from wallpapers. Choosing No image removes the Launch image.
Its image fills only this tile, in both layouts.
Save and reload applies the changes. The boot colours
are visible at the next Hekate boot.

On first launch: Carbon, Nova colours, window transparency
75% (opacity 25%), original date/time setting and language choice.
The existing preferences remain priority. Reset makes it possible to
return to the Nova values; this choice must then be saved.

DIAGNOSTIC
The complete test opens a summary of 15 components/sets with measured
results, coverage and troubleshooting leads. The Summary per component button
allows it to be reopened from the results. The original reports remain
accessible. Export also saves the summary and the last additional checks
of the session in bootloader/diagnostics.

Commands: Joy-Con/Lite test with buttons memorised, rest/amplitude of the
sticks, measured offset and losses/reconnections. POWER allows exiting.
Touch: grid over the whole screen; POWER finishes. The zones not covered
are not tested, and are not declared faulty.
Screen: red, green, blue, white and black test patterns; VOL+/VOL- changes the pattern,
POWER exits. Touching the background hides the commands. The observations are
manual and are kept in the summary of the session.
RAM: fast, advanced or custom modes with at least one pattern. The
program launches separately and returns automatically to Hekate. Its
result indicates the completed patterns, the zone read back, the errors and the duration.
The first 4 MiB remain reserved. Unstable hardware may prevent the
return. Export after a complete test includes the returned RAM result.

PASS only concerns the controls performed. An identification alone does
not certify all the functions of a chip. The fault leads are not
replacement instructions. The interactive observations are not
kept after a restart: export the report to keep them.

DISPLAY
Boot logo centred and bar brought closer. Progress by steps,
animated during the waits of the drivers that query the time. No
new hardware interrupt; animation suspended during the last memory
training and stopped before the transfer to Nyx.
White reflection: 240 ms travel, pauses alternating 3 and 5 seconds.
The blue/purple border remains animated. The effects can be disabled.
The waves/particles background animations remain removed.

VERIFICATIONS
Hekate/Nyx compilation and RAM service, PC tests of the real graphics engine,
of the seven languages, of the save and of the error paths, of the RAM
protocols and of the reports. See documentation/VERIFICATION-V17.json and
documentation/SUIVI-CAHIER-CHARGES-V17.md.
Physical validation on Switch not carried out. The redrawn surfaces
measured on PC do not give the real FPS of the console.
