# Hekate Nova Pro 19-TV — first integration of the TV output

This version takes Nova Pro v18 and adds a manual TV output that remains
active in the menus. Base Hekate 6.5.4 / Nyx 1.9.4, Nova interface preserved.
The standalone prototype 01, based on 6.5.5, remains available separately.

## What has been validated

You confirmed a stable image and the stop with VOL- with prototype 01 on
your Switch OLED inserted directly into an official dock. The reported failures
used a cable between the console and the dock. They are therefore not considered
a confirmed failure of the driver in a direct connection. The exact type
of this cable and its connection have not been identified.

This Nova integration is new: it still has to be tried on console.
Its PC tests do not replace that trial. The video driver and its parameters
remain identical to those of prototype 01; the transitions in Nova are new.

## Installation

1. Back up your current `bootloader` folder, or use a test SD card.
2. Copy the `bootloader` folder provided in this archive to the root.
   The personal configuration files `hekate_ipl.ini` and `nyx.ini`
   are not provided, in order to keep your preferences.
3. Inject `hekate_nova_pro_6.5.4_v19_tv.bin`. Check in the credits that the
   Nova version indicated is **19-TV**. Copying only the payload without the
   files of `bootloader/sys` does not update Nyx.

## Enabling the TV and returning to the menus

1. Connect the official dock to its power supply and to the television via HDMI.
2. Open **Nyx Settings → TV / Dock → TV USB-C / Dock**.
   The quick settings button at the top right opens the Nyx settings.
3. Choose **Enable the TV** and insert the console directly into the dock.
   You may launch outside the dock: you have 45 seconds to put it in place.
4. After connection, close this window. The TV output remains active on the home screen,
   the tabs and the Nova windows. The mode is 1280 × 720 at 60 Hz.
5. **VOL-** stops the TV output at any time between the initialisation phases.
   The **Stop the TV** button is also available by reopening its settings.
6. Removing the console or losing the dock signal stops the session. To resume
   it, put the console back and use **Enable the TV** again.

The console screen remains on. The existing Nyx controls are kept,
including the pointer with the Joy-Con on the rails when this option is active.
This version does not add the Bluetooth connection of the detached controllers.

## Stop before another hardware function

Nova cuts the TV output before a system or payload launch, a
reload, a power-off/restart, an active hardware diagnostic or a storage/controller
USB use. The TV buffer is released after the display engine
has stopped. The menu frequency is restored after the dock negotiation.

An active diagnostic can therefore return to the console screen. The result
remains accessible in Nova; you can then re-enable the TV manually.
The memory test keeps its separate payload and its existing return to Hekate.

HDMI audio, the automatic activation at startup and the connection with an
intermediary cable are not validated by this delivery.

## Reports

The reports are written on the SD in `bootloader/tv-test/`:

- `last-run.txt`: last trial;
- `run-001.txt`, `run-002.txt`, etc.: separate copy of each trial, up to 999.

An error of mine in the 01/02 prototypes prevented the normal writing
of the reports: the result of `sd_mount()` was interpreted the wrong way round.
This version uses the real BDK contract, verified separately on PC.
There is therefore no need to look for a missing log from prototype 01.

To validate this integration, check the image on the home screen after closing the
TV window, the opening of a few submenus, VOL-, then a reconnection.
In case of a problem, attach the report of the trial with what appears on screen.

## Provenance and rollback

The source archive contains the exact files used, the tests, their
results, the provenance of the drivers and the Hekate licence. See
`documentation/TV-SOURCES.md` before any public redistribution.

Nova v18 and the 01/02 prototypes are kept in their previous archives.
To go back, restore the saved `bootloader` folder and launch
the corresponding payload. No modification of your `Desktop/nd5` source folder
was made during this development.
