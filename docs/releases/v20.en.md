# Hekate Nova Pro 20-Dock

This version adds automatic dock detection to the TV functions of
Nova19-TV. The reference connection is a Switch OLED inserted directly
into an official Nintendo dock, with power supply and HDMI connected.
The automatic activation of this version still has to be tried on console.

## Install

Back up your `bootloader` folder, or use a test SD card. Copy the
provided `bootloader` folder to the root, then launch
`hekate_nova_pro_6.5.4_v20_dock.bin`. Check **20-Dock** in the credits.
The package keeps your personal `nyx.ini` and `hekate_ipl.ini` files.
Nyx is in `bootloader/sys/nyx.bin`: replacing only the payload is not enough.

## Automatic activation: ON / OFF

In **Nyx Settings → TV / Dock**, enable **Automatic dock detection**,
then use **Save** to keep your choice after a restart.

- **ON**: when Nyx starts already on the dock, or when you subsequently place
  the console on it, the TV connection starts automatically after a short
  presence check. The TV must be switched on and on the correct HDMI input.
- **OFF**: no automatic detection in the background. The **Enable
  the TV** button remains available. Disabling the automation does not cut an already
  started TV connection: use **Stop the TV** or **VOL-** for that.

The new option is disabled by default in order to keep the behaviour
of the previous configurations. Once saved, it is re-read at each
Nyx startup. It does not modify the TV functions of an already launched system.

The detection waits about two seconds after the interface initialisation,
then confirms the presence of the dock on two separate reads. The negotiation
and the initialisation of the video then take their own time.

## Console screen

The **TV display** setting is also in the same tab:

- **TV + console** keeps both screens; this is the initial setting.
- **TV only** switches off the console screen after the successful initialisation of the TV.
  It switches back on when the TV output is stopped or unplugged.

Use **Save** to keep this choice. The brightness set in Nyx
is kept for the return to the console screen. It is not overwritten
by the switch to TV.

## Stop, removal and new attempts

**VOL-** or **Stop the TV** cuts the output. The automation does not switch it back on
immediately: remove the console then put it back on the dock to re-arm the
detection, or use **Enable the TV** manually.

An automatic attempt that fails is not repeated in a loop. If the TV or
the HDMI was not ready, check the connection then relaunch manually or
remove/replace the console. A controller read error is not
interpreted as a disconnection.

The detector recognises the usual power supply offer of Nintendo docks,
then the driver performs the complete negotiation. The other chargers/adapters
do not constitute the reference connection; manual activation remains
available. The Switch Lite is not supported for TV output.

The hardware diagnostics and the exclusive USB functions suspend the TV
service. Their return restores the automatic monitoring; a TV output that was
active can be re-enabled. The automation respects a previous manual stop.
A system launch, payload launch, power-off or restart terminates the service.

## Reports and validation

The reports are in `bootloader/tv-test/last-run.txt` and `run-NNN.txt`.
For the trial on console, check:

1. ON saved, restart of Nyx with the console on the dock;
2. startup outside the dock then placing on the dock;
3. stop with VOL-, absence of immediate relaunch, then removal/reconnection;
4. return to the console screen and retention of the choices after a restart.

The compilation and the PC tests check the code, the preferences and the error
cases. They do not replace these physical trials. The video driver and its
settings are kept from the prototype01 tested in direct insertion.
The HDMI audio output and the intermediary cables are not validated here.

## Wireless Joy-Con

They are not enabled in this version. The current code tests the radio,
but the Bluetooth service for connecting and controlling the controllers is missing.
See `documentation/ETUDE-JOYCONS-SANS-FIL.md` for the analysis and the references.
The Joy-Con on rails keep their existing support.

The Nova19-TV and prototype01 archives are kept to return to the
previous version. See `documentation/TV-SOURCES.md` for the provenance.
