# Hekate Nova Pro 30 — Bluetooth in the background and smoothness

At the startup of Nova, the driver checks the physical presence on the rails.

- No Joy-Con attached: loading of `bootloader/bluetooth/joycon-pairing.bin`,
  then automatic activation of Bluetooth. The `.bak` copy is tried if
  necessary. The service starts from the home screen without opening a window.
- At least one Joy-Con attached: no SD loading nor automatic Bluetooth
  startup. The usual operation of the rails is kept.
- No valid pairing recorded: the startup continues with Bluetooth
  stopped, without radio error nor blocking window.

This decision is made once per startup, after initialisation of the
rails. An attachment detected during the SD reading cancels the automatic
startup. The manual stop is respected. Detaching a Joy-Con afterwards does not
relaunch the service automatically in the same session. The
disabled driver and the models without rails supported by this driver are excluded
from this automatic startup.

The v29 saves are compatible. Use the one corresponding to
this console and its controllers, in particular for the trials on two OLED. If they do not exist yet,
attach the controllers, read the pairings and save them once from
the Wireless Joy-Con menu, Bluetooth stopped. The `.bin` and `.bak` files
contain private keys: do not attach them to the reports.

The service works in the background on the home screen and the ordinary menus.
A press on a normal button may remain necessary to wake a
switched-off controller. The hardware tests keep their necessary suspensions
during the measurements; v30 does not force their radio to remain busy.

The audit led to the following optimisations:

- Reflection: avoids the periodic overflows of the drawing queue, while
  keeping its appearance. Reduction of the redrawn surfaces verified on PC.
- Commands: keeps once the short presses received between two reads,
  with expiration so as not to replay an old command after a freeze.
- Maintenance: the fast polling continues at the maintenance points of the long
  tools, outside suspended diagnostics.
- Measurements: the report includes the maximum full service time, its variants,
  the interval between calls and the number of RX slices at the ceiling.
- Resources: check of the sizes and of the reads of `res.pak`; cleaning
  of the refused payloads, verification before allocation and complete reading.

Installation: merge the `bootloader` folder of the ZIP with the one of the SD,
replacing the provided files in order to keep your private save,
then restart.
Use `hekate_nova_pro_6.5.5_v30.bin` if necessary. No SD formatting nor
replacement of your settings is necessary. The keys saved on the
card are not provided nor erased by this distribution.

After the trial, "Stop Bluetooth" records
`bootloader/bluetooth/last-session.txt`. `Autostart=2` means that the automatic
launch was requested; it does not prove that the controllers are already
awake and connected. `Autostart=1` indicates the presence on the rails,
`3` the absence of a valid save and `5` an unavailable detection.
The polling maxima describe the service on the host, not the complete latency
between a physical button and the image. No hardware FPS target is announced.

Compilation and PC tests are to be completed by a trial on the two OLED.
Base: Hekate6.5.5 / Nyx1.9.5. Licences and audit reports included.
