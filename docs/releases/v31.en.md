# Hekate Nova Pro — beta v31

Base: Hekate 6.5.5 / Nyx 1.9.5. This archive contains the complete bootloader folder and the corresponding payload.

## Expert partitioning menu

- Three settings across the full width, horizontal summary at the bottom.
- Longer sliders and −/+ buttons with repeat on held press.
- Sizes displayed in whole GB, without decimals. "29 GB Full" and "58 GB Full" markers, also for the double emuMMC.
- No modification of the real sizes, reservations or alignments: the displayed GB are rounded down to the lower integer, like the Hekate markers. Android displays its total allocation, system included. The Hekate emuMMC steps are kept; Linux and Android use the 1 GB steps after their minimum.
- The existing erasure confirmations remain active. The modification of the menu launches no partitioning.
- Nova theme kept, including the wallpaper and the transparency.

## Bluetooth and smoothness

The Bluetooth processing continues during the long software drawings, the image copies and the display rotation waits. The transition from the drawing is limited to a reception budget of 250 µs, at most once every 2 ms; the processing of a whole packet may exceed this budget. It launches neither another menu, nor a graphics task, nor a write on the SD.

The operation of v30 is kept: automatic startup from the saved pairing file when both rails are empty at startup, without opening the Bluetooth menu. The presence of a Joy-Con on a rail prevents this automation. A manual stop remains respected until the next startup. The diagnostics that reserve the peripherals keep their dedicated operation.

This version adds read-only link measurements, spaced (at most two requests per second in total). No power, EDR, UART speed, pairing or encryption setting has been changed. No additional HCD firmware is loaded.

**The range beyond 80 cm is not yet validated on console.** The v30 report shows a maximum of 118.5 ms between two processing passes on the host side and a longer reception interruption on the right; it does not prove a radio failure nor the exact cause of the distance. This beta improves the processing possibilities and makes it possible to measure what remains.

## Installation

1. Console switched off, keep a copy of your bootloader folder and of your personal files.
2. Merge the bootloader folder of this archive with the one of the SD, replacing the provided files. Keep the new Nyx and the corresponding payload together.
3. Use hekate_nova_pro_6.5.5_v31.bin if your boot method uses an external payload. bootloader/update.bin is also provided.
4. Keep bootloader/bluetooth/joycon-pairing.bin or its .bak backup: this update does not change their format. Do not clear the keys nor clean the whole SD for this trial.

No private pairing file and no personal configuration file are included in the archive.

## Recommended trial

At an identical console position, test a session nearby then another one beyond 80 cm. In each session, move the sticks and press A/B for about 20 seconds. Choose "Stop Bluetooth" and copy immediately bootloader/bluetooth/last-session.txt before relaunching or reading the pairings again. Keep the two reports under distinct names.

Send only these text reports; the .bin/.bak pairing files contain private keys.

The report adds:
- Capture_ms and number of passes during the drawing.
- Full_reports, age of the last full report, maximum between full reports and counters of gaps >50 / >250 ms. The acknowledgements are not counted as full reports. The session statistics do not constitute a button-to-screen latency measurement.
- RF kind=0: relative BR/EDR RSSI in dB; 0 means within the controller's range, not a null signal. It is not an absolute measurement in dBm.
- kind=1: vendor quality from 0 to 255, not a percentage.
- kind=2/3: current / maximum transmit power in dBm, only read.
- kind=4: counter of consecutive failed contacts of the controller, not a total of all the lost packets.
- For each measurement: number of samples, age, last value and session minimum/maximum. n/a means no valid measurement. Old values do not necessarily describe the link at the time of the export.
- Measurement errors: FD = power value out of range, FE = missing response, FF = malformed response or wrong identifier. An unsupported measurement or one without response is disabled for this connection; it does not stop the commands. These codes are independent of the authentication errors.
