## Bluetooth: recovery of a silent Joy-Con

When an already established full stream remains silent for one second, Nova requests the full report mode again. If the silence lasts four seconds, Nova requests a disconnection of this Joy-Con only and attempts a new connection with the existing pairings, one second after the effective HID closing. Without a first full report, a margin of eight seconds leaves the time for the initial configuration.

The other Joy-Con continues its processing. The recovery does not restart the radio and does not modify the keys. It waits for the end of the UART data already being read: a partial packet or a reached reading budget must not trigger this recovery. The simple mode, which sends its changes on demand, is not treated as a missing periodic stream. The manual stop and the automatic startup of v31 remain respected.

The initialisation also keeps WL_REG_ON high during the Bluetooth session, as the HWTest-RCM reference does after its common reset. The previous level is restored on stop, whether it was high or low. No Wi-Fi network service is started. This modification brings the sequence closer to the reference; its effect on the range is to be checked on the console.

**The target of 2–3 metres remains a hardware trial to be carried out. This beta does not yet guarantee this range.** The received v31 report indicates report cuts up to 2.8 s, whereas the maximum processing on the host side is about 22 ms. It also presents negative relative RSSI and a drop in the vendor link quality. These data point towards the radio link; they do not prove a precise hardware cause nor a gain brought by v32.

No forced power increase, EDR/UART modification or HCD firmware load is applied. The radio remains in ROM. The HCI measurements added in v31 remain available. A connection recovery improves the recovery after an interruption; it does not by itself increase the signal range.

The v32 report adds Radio_power_policy, as well as Stream report_restarts / reconnects per Joy-Con and the recovery events (kind=21). These counters distinguish the recovery requests from the reconnections actually completed; the presence of new reports and HID=1 makes it possible to confirm the return of the commands. The counters are kept during the export, until the next Bluetooth startup.

## Installation and trial

1. Console switched off, keep a copy of bootloader and of the personal files.
2. Merge the bootloader folder of the archive with the one of the SD, replacing the provided files. Keep the personal configuration and pairing files.
3. Use hekate_nova_pro_6.5.5_v32.bin if the boot uses an external payload. bootloader/update.bin is also provided.
4. Keep bootloader/bluetooth/joycon-pairing.bin and its .bak backup: format unchanged, no re-reading or deletion of keys necessary to update.

Test the sticks and A/B nearby, then at 2 m and 3 m keeping the console in the same place. A prolonged command stop can now cause an automatic reconnection of the Joy-Con concerned. After each session, choose "Stop Bluetooth" and keep last-session.txt immediately under a distinct name. Indicate whether the console is in the dock.

Send only the text reports; the .bin/.bak pairing files contain private keys. The archive contains no private pairing data nor personal configuration.
