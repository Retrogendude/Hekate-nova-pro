# Hekate Nova Pro — v12 fixes
**Languages:** [Français](CORRECTIONS-NOVA-PRO-V12.md) · **English** · [Español](CORRECTIONS-NOVA-PRO-V12.es.md) · [Русский](CORRECTIONS-NOVA-PRO-V12.ru.md)

Hekate 6.5.4 / Nyx 1.9.4. This delivery builds on Nova v11 and the seven requested fixes. Point 7 follows the latest instruction: **keep the original time setting and simply add the language to it**.

1. **Wallpaper behind the menus.** The wallpaper is a protected, non-interactive child, explicitly placed at the back plane. The screen itself has its own opaque base colour; the intermediate opaque container is removed. The windows also place their own background behind their content. The trials now reproduce a real container screen and the six main pages built together.
2. **Independent colours.** Nyx Settings > Themes and colours > Colours offers Background, Texts, Outlines, Logo, Icons and Selection. The last three colours are saved separately, in 24-bit RGB. The sliders and the samples show a preview; Save and reload applies the choices. The glyphs and the selection controls use these settings instead of the old global tint. The opacity percentages concern only the surfaces.
3. **Diagonal white reflection.** The outline animations are removed. A soft white band crosses only the symbol of the selected card, following its transparency. It passes in about 1.2 seconds then rests for about 2.4 seconds. No iridescent colour, background animation or blurry shadow. The masks are small (40 × 40 maximum), allocated at selection; the timer allocates nothing. It stops during the diagnostics and goes on hold during the interactions. Selection effects makes it possible to disable the reflection. The historical key nova_halo is preserved; nova_reflection_color remains readable for compatibility but no longer colours the white reflection.
4. **Logo to the credits.** A press on the Hekate Nova Pro monogram opens the credits directly. The four commands at the top right remain available with the usual confirmations.
5. **Coloured temperature.** Only the temperature changes colour; the time keeps its style. Green below 45 °C, yellow from 45 to less than 55 °C, orange from 55 to less than 65 °C, red from 65 °C. These ranges serve as visual markers, not as guaranteed operating limits. The fan management and the hardware protections remain the original ones.
6. **Nova switches.** The settings use tracks and sliders with little rounding, thin outlines and explicit Active/Inactive states. The active state follows the Icons and Selection colours. The actions, confirmations and original restrictions are preserved.
7. **Original time + language.** The original trigger, when no time has yet been validated, always opens the same window: year, month, day, hour, minutes and daylight saving time. Seven language buttons are added only to this first setting. Cancel does not validate the language. Save and reload saves time and language then reloads Nyx. Installations with a time already configured are not forced to redo this screen. The manual setting of the clock remains available in its usual place. No second wizard nor new first-installation marker. The date is always a display offset: the hardware clock shared with the system is not rewritten.

## Backup

The new colours use nova_logo_color, nova_icon_color and nova_selection_color in bootloader/nyx.ini. The old fields and their position in memory are preserved; no INI file is supplied in the archive. A save first writes and closes nyx.ini.nova.tmp, then replaces the final file with a recovery copy nyx.ini.nova.bak. If the replacement fails, the program tries to restore the old file. At boot, a recovery copy is used when the main file is absent. Write or closing errors prevent the replacement. An error during the first setting keeps the window and makes it possible to try again.

## Checks and limits

Build with devkitARM, check of the binary limits, verification of the 42 probes and of the RAM companion. The trials on PC execute the real LVGL code: seven languages, nine sizes, four font families, backgrounds and transparencies, selection in two presses and interactions. The clock actions are compared to a calendar and to reference dates, including leap years. The SD, write, close and rename errors are simulated.

The detailed reports are delivered in verification/. **No trial of this version on Switch has been carried out here.** The measurements of redrawn surfaces are not measurements of the FPS of the console. Check on the device the wallpaper, the reflection, the colours, the real temperatures and the first setting before considering the version validated in hardware.
