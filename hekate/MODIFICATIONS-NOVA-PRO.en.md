# Hekate Nova Pro — requested changes
**Languages:** [Français](MODIFICATIONS-NOVA-PRO.md) · **English** · [Español](MODIFICATIONS-NOVA-PRO.es.md) · [Русский](MODIFICATIONS-NOVA-PRO.ru.md)

1. Battery percentage on the right, next to its icon, including after a refresh.
2. Navigation at the top left; direct accesses Nyx, reload, restart and power off on the right.
3. Animated luminous reflection on the outlines of the selection and of its icon, with a limited display cost.
4. Remove the badges/banners from the five main menus.
5. Apply the Nova visual language to all the windows and to the sub-menus.
6. Tools, Info, Diagnostic: first press = selection; second press on the same tile = opening. Remove the opening arrow.
7. Themes: independent colours for the background, the text, the outlines and the reflection.
8. Respect the layouts, proportions and components of the Nova mock-up in the sub-menus.
9. Boot "Hekate Nova Pro" and new loading animation.
10. New original logo, replacing the four bars of the home screen.
11. Choice of a fixed wallpaper and separate transparencies for the menu and icon backgrounds.
12. Remove the background animations as well as their settings; keep the selection reflection.

Version 10 and its archives remain available in outputs. The rendering checks on computer do not replace the trials on console.

The twelve points are applied in version 11 and the build is validated. The software checks and the 4 260 rendering scenes pass. The trials of this version on console still have to be carried out.
